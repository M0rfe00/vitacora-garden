# Prerequisites
___
- Before attending this course, students must have:
- An understanding of networking fundamentals and virtualization
- Familiarity Networking and the TCP/IP core layers
- Basic knowledge of Linux / Microsoft operating systems
- Comprehension of simple aerodynamics
- An eagerness to learn with an open mind
