## Safety Basics:
Batteries (Litium)
- Energy dense like hydrogen, gasoline or TNT
- Similarly can realease its energy quickly
- High current discharges can also cause burns and destroy parts

Propellers
- Highest operational requierment to cost ratio
- Sharp when turning (Pictured systems spins at>40k rpm)
- Always remove propellers when working on an UAV


## US Rules and Laws (Summary)
FAA handles flight outdoors - FAA.gov
- Part 107 is for commercial use (traditionally during the day, under 55 lbs AUW)
- All drones over 250g (1/2 pound or a can of soda) must be registered
FCC Rules - FCC.gov
- "Part 15" (47 CFR Part 15) covers RF operations for unlicensed operators
- Amateur Radio (47 CFR Part 97) covers RF operations for licensed operators
Local Enforcement
- Counties / Cities / Parks may have their own rules about SUAS operation
- The model relies on enforcement by regional police forces

## CLASS RULES
- Only operate experimental aircraft in controlled and confined spaces.
- Keep propellers off until the aircraft is ready to fly.
- Do not leave charging batteries unattended.
- Do not operate the aircraft until the instruction states to do so; otherwise damage to aircraft, user, and environment may occur.
- All operators must land aircraft upon hearing 'Aircraft Down'.
- Operators must verbally announce flights before takeoff.
- Do not operate the aircraft outside without appropriate licensure, verifying airspace, and following all local rules and laws.
