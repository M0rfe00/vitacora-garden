# AUTONOMOUS VS. AUTONOMY
___
- Autonomy: An operational state where equipment can perform programmed operations under defined conditions without human input or guidance (e.g. the UAV is able to detect contested airspace and modify operational objectives and plans to accommodate the interruption)

- Automation: Refers to a set of related functions performed automatically by equipment (e.g. the UAV maintains a stable location, heading, and altitude using internal sensors)

The more autonomous a system is, the less RF bandwidth it needs for operation.
