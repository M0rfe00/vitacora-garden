# **ASTROLOGY & ASTRONOMY**
___
Astrology and astronomy are not the same thing, although they are connected; astrology is an ancient practice that studies how the movements of the celestial bodies might influence people’s lives and events on Earth, On the other hand, astronomy is a science that focuses on understanding the universe, the planets, the stars, and celestial phenomena from a purely physical and mathematical perspective. 

In ancient times, the two were closely linked because observing the sky was key to both disciplines, but over time they took different paths: astronomy remained rooted in science, while astrology focused on symbolic interpretation.
